# Prompt Examples

Use these examples to learn what you can ask Jarvis. These are just a very smalll set of examples of the commands that can be used.  

## Morning Briefing

The morning briefing gives you a quick summary of your day. Jarvis checks your saved emails, today’s calendar, today’s weather, and may include a helpful suggestion based on saved memory.

### Command: Morning Briefing

Use this when you want Jarvis to give you a full daily overview.

**Examples:** 
Hey Jarvis, morning briefing.

Hey Jarvis, give me my morning briefing.


### Command: Daily Briefing

Use this as another way to ask for your daily overview.

**Examples:** 
Hey Jarvis, daily briefing.
 
Hey Jarvis, give me my daily briefing.


### Command: Start My Day

Use this when you want Jarvis to prepare your morning summary.

**Examples:** 
Hey Jarvis, start my day.
 
Hey Jarvis, help me start my day.


### Command: Brief Me

Use this when you want a short briefing without saying the full command.

**Examples:** 
Hey Jarvis, brief me.
 
Hey Jarvis, brief me on my day.


### Command: Day Overview

Use this when you want to know what your day looks like.

**Examples:** 
Hey Jarvis, what's my day look like?
 
Hey Jarvis, what does my day look like?


### Command: Give Me My Briefing

Use this as a natural way to request your full briefing.

**Examples:** 
Hey Jarvis, give me my briefing.
 
Hey Jarvis, can you give me my briefing?


## Calendar

Jarvis can check your calendar, tell you what is coming up, add events, remove events, and update scheduled appointments.

### Command: Check Calendar

Use this when you want to know what is on your schedule.

**Examples:** 
Hey Jarvis, what's on my calendar today?
 
Hey Jarvis, what do I have tomorrow?


### Command: Check Agenda

Use this when you want Jarvis to read your agenda for a specific day.

**Examples:**
 
Hey Jarvis, what's on my agenda today?

 
Hey Jarvis, what are my plans for tomorrow?


### Command: Check Availability

Use this when you want to know if your calendar is open or busy.

**Examples:** 
Hey Jarvis, am I free today?
 
Hey Jarvis, am I busy tomorrow afternoon?


### Command: Add Calendar Event

Use this when you want Jarvis to schedule something on your calendar.

**Examples:** 
Hey Jarvis, add a calendar event for dinner with Lindsay tomorrow at 6 PM.
 
Hey Jarvis, schedule a meeting with John on Friday at 2 PM.


### Command: Set Reminder

Use this when you want Jarvis to put a reminder or appointment on your calendar.

**Examples:** 
Hey Jarvis, remind me to call Mom tomorrow at 5 PM.
 
Hey Jarvis, put down dentist appointment next Tuesday at 9 AM.


### Command: Cancel Calendar Event

Use this when you want Jarvis to remove an upcoming event.

**Examples:**
 
Hey Jarvis, cancel my dentist appointment.
 
Hey Jarvis, remove the meeting with John from my calendar.


### Command: Delete Last Mentioned Event

Use this after Jarvis reads your schedule and you want to remove the event it just mentioned.

**Examples:**
 
Hey Jarvis, delete that one.
 
Hey Jarvis, cancel that event.


### Command: Reschedule Event

Use this when you want Jarvis to move an event to a new time.

**Examples:** 
Hey Jarvis, reschedule my dentist appointment to Friday at 10 AM.
 
Hey Jarvis, move my meeting with John to tomorrow at 3 PM.


### Command: Update Calendar Event

Use this when you want to change the name or time of an event.

**Examples:** 
Hey Jarvis, update my meeting with John to project planning.
 
Hey Jarvis, change my appointment to 4 PM.

## Email

Jarvis can check your saved new emails, read email summaries out loud, and send emails using your saved contacts.

### Command: Check Email

Use this when you want Jarvis to check for new saved emails.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, check my email.

Hey Jarvis, check my emails.


### Command: Read Email

Use this when you want Jarvis to read your new email summaries.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, read my emails.


### Command: Latest Email

Use this when you want Jarvis to tell you about the newest saved emails.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, do I have any latest emails?

Hey Jarvis, any new emails?


### Command: Read Messages

Use this as another natural way to ask Jarvis to read your saved mail.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, read my messages.

Hey Jarvis, read my mail.


### Command: Send Email

Use this when you want Jarvis to start the email sending flow.

Jarvis will ask who the email should go to, what the subject should be, and what message you want to send.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, send an email.

Hey Jarvis, send email.


### Command: Send Email To Contact

Use this when you already know who you want to email. Jarvis will try to match the spoken name to your saved contacts.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, send an email to Lindsay.

Hey Jarvis, email John.


### Command: Cancel Email

Use this during the email sending flow if you change your mind.

**Examples:**
Cancel.

Nevermind.

## Research

Jarvis can run background research, check the status of active research, cancel research, resume unfinished research, read completed reports, and answer follow-up questions about completed research.

### Command: Start Research

Use this when you want Jarvis to research a topic in the background.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, start research on the best open source text to speech tools.

Hey Jarvis, do research about local AI assistants.


### Command: Run Research

Use this as another way to start a research task.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, run research on privacy friendly home assistant tools.

Hey Jarvis, begin research about using local LLMs for voice assistants.


### Command: Research Why

Use this when you want Jarvis to investigate the reason behind something.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, research why Ollama is using so much GPU.

Hey Jarvis, research why local speech to text is slow on Windows.


### Command: Research Status

Use this when you want to know how the active research task is going.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, research status.

Hey Jarvis, how is the research going?


### Command: Current Research

Use this when you want to know what Jarvis is currently researching.

Hey Jarvis, what are you researching?

Hey Jarvis, what research are you doing?


### Command: Cancel Research

Use this when you want Jarvis to stop the active research task.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, cancel research.

Hey Jarvis, stop the research.


### Command: Resume Research

Use this when Jarvis restarted or you want it to continue unfinished research.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, resume research.

Hey Jarvis, finish unfinished research.


### Command: Finish Any Task Not Complete

Use this when you want Jarvis to check for unfinished research and continue anything still active.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, finish any task not complete.

Hey Jarvis, continue unfinished research.

### Command: Read Research Report

Use this when you want Jarvis to read a completed research report about a specific topic.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, read research on local AI assistants.

Hey Jarvis, research report on open source text to speech.


### Command: What Did You Find

Use this when you want Jarvis to summarize a completed research topic.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, what did you find on local LLMs?

Hey Jarvis, what did you find about open source TTS?


### Command: Completed Research

Use this when you want Jarvis to pull up a saved completed research report.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, completed research on privacy friendly AI assistants.

Hey Jarvis, show research on Ollama GPU usage.


### Command: Ask About Completed Research

Use this after Jarvis says research is complete. Jarvis can answer follow-up questions using the completed report.

**Examples:**
What were the most important findings?

Can you summarize the report?


### Command: Exit Research Questions

Use this when you are done asking follow-up questions about a completed research report.

**Examples:**
Done with research.

That's all.

### Command: Show completed research on UI and Close

Use this when you want to view completed research on the UI

**Examples:**
Show completed research

Close completed research

## Weather

Jarvis can check the current weather, today’s weather, tomorrow’s forecast, the upcoming week, or weather for a specific date.

### Command: Current Weather

Use this when you want to know the weather right now.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, what's the weather in Pittsburgh PA?

Hey Jarvis, weather in New York New York


### Command: Today's Weather

Use this when you want the weather for today.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, what's the weather in Pittsburgh PA today?

Hey Jarvis, give me today's weather in Miami Flordia.


### Command: Tomorrow's Weather

Use this when you want tomorrow’s forecast.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, what's the weather tomorrow in Pittsburgh PA?

Hey Jarvis, weather in Boardman Ohio tomorrow.


### Command: Weekly Forecast

Use this when you want a forecast for the upcoming week.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, what's the weather this week in Pittsburgh PA?

Hey Jarvis, give me the 7 day weather in Pittsburgh Pennsylvania.


### Command: Weather By Date

Use this when you want the forecast for a specific date.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, what's the weather in Pittsburgh PA on June 20?

Hey Jarvis, weather in Pittsburgh PA on 07/04.


### Command: Follow-Up Weather

Use this after asking about a location. Jarvis can remember the last weather city and state during the current session.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, what's the weather in Pittsburgh PA today?

Hey Jarvis, what about tomorrow?

## Ask Jarvis About Itself

You can ask Jarvis questions about how it works, what features it has, or how parts of the system are built.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, what can you do?

Hey Jarvis, explain how your wake word works.

Hey Jarvis, how does your memory work?

## General Chat

You can ask Jarvis normal questions or have a regular conversation. If your request does not match a specific tool like weather, email, calendar, or research, Jarvis will answer using the local LLM.

**Examples:**
Hey Jarvis, explain what an LLM is.

Hey Jarvis, give me three ideas for improving my project.

Hey Jarvis, is Batman the best super hero!
